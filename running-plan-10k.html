// Key principles for 10K
🐢
Long runs stay slow
Your Sunday long run should be slower than your 5K pace. Comfortable enough to hold a full conversation the whole way.
📈
10% rule
Weekly mileage shouldn't jump more than ~10% at a time. The recovery weeks are non-negotiable — don't skip them.
🔁
Repeat if needed
If a week felt like a struggle, repeat it. Consistency over weeks matters far more than rushing to the next step.
💧
Fuel on longer runs
Once your long run passes 60 minutes, consider taking water and a small amount of carbohydrate (a gel or a banana) during the run.
😴
Sleep is training
Aerobic adaptation happens during sleep. Prioritise 7–9 hours in the nights after your harder sessions.
🏁
Race day pacing
Start slower than you think you need to. The first 3K should feel easy. You'll have plenty left for the second half.
